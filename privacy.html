Privacy Notice

This page describes how the Recountix public marketing website currently handles information. It is a basic website notice, not a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy for a full production service.

Information you enter

The demo request form on this website runs in your browser and opens your default email application with the information you entered, addressed to support.recountix@gmail.com. This static page does not send the form directly to a Recountix server or store the form submission in a website database. When you choose to send that email, your email provider will process it and the Recountix team may receive its contents.

Website analytics and cookies

This page does not intentionally set its own analytics cookies or provide a direct form-submission backend. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may result in requests to Google's services. Hosting providers and third-party services may process technical request information under their own policies.

Contact

For questions about this notice, email support.recountix@gmail.com.

Changes and scope

This notice describes the public marketing page only. It does not describe data processing inside any separate Recountix application, account, database, or service. Before adding accounts, analytics, or a backend form, update this notice to accurately explain the actual data collected, purposes, retention, sharing, and user choices.
